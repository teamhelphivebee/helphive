Frequently asked questions

How do I contact HelpHive?

Email teamhelphive@gmail.com from the address on your account. In the app, you can also reach us from Profile → Contact us.

How do bookings and payment work?

Parents search for a sitter by date and time, send a booking request, and chat with the sitter once it's accepted. Sitting fees and the HelpHive membership are for real-world childcare arranged through the app — nothing digital is unlocked by a payment.

Who can be a sitter on HelpHive?

Sitters are college students who verify their student status with a .edu email address and complete our onboarding and background-check process before their profile goes live.

How do I report or block someone?

Long-press a chat message, or tap the overflow menu (⋮) on a sitter profile or review, and choose Report. To stop a specific person from contacting you or appearing in your search results, use Block from that same menu, or manage existing blocks from Profile → Blocked accounts. Every report is sent to our team for review.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. You can also request deletion from the web at helphiveco.com/delete-account.html without opening the app.

How do I cancel my membership?

Email us at teamhelphive@gmail.com and we'll cancel it for you — deleting your account also cancels any active membership.

I found a bug or something isn't working

Let us know what happened, what device you're using, and if possible, a screenshot — it helps us track it down faster.
